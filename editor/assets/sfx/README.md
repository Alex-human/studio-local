# Image click

`image-click-dulce-seda.wav` is a softer treatment of **Standard Click**, asset #1 from CreatorAssets' **10 Click Sound Effects** pack.

- Source: https://creatorassets.com/audio/10-clicks/
- Source license: CC0 / public domain, as published on the source page on 2026-09-18.
- Local treatment: cropped, levelled, low/high-shelf EQ, a narrow cut around 2.9 kHz, 8.3 kHz low-pass and gentle soft compression.
- WAV SHA-256: `d53c3a4174130170a7a71a9ab48428240ec279644d6ed3313a939d835919775f`

Studio applies another 6 dB of internal headroom before the image's `soundDb` and SFX mixer gains.
